Desktop Please Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 5, 2026

Desktop Please is developed by Jack Campbell. The app and Safari extension do not collect personal data or send browsing information to the developer. They contain no advertising, analytics, tracking code, or user account system.

Safari extension and local preferences

The extension requests access to reddit.com and redd.it so Safari can apply rules that change the browser identification header sent to those websites. It does not read page contents, record browsing history, or access passwords. Safari stores your on/off preference for the extension’s rules.

Clipboard

The setup app writes a public Reddit link to your clipboard only when you tap “Copy Reddit test link.” It does not read your clipboard.

Third-party services

When you browse Reddit, your browser communicates directly with Reddit under Reddit’s own privacy practices. Desktop Please does not control Reddit’s data collection. Apple may separately process App Store or device diagnostics according to your settings and Apple’s policies.

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to operate the hosting service. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Support requests

If you email for support, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond and troubleshoot. Support correspondence is handled through Gmail and may be retained as needed to resolve and follow up on the request. Please do not send passwords or sensitive browsing information. You can request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the address below.

Your choices

You can disable the extension in Safari settings or uninstall Desktop Please. Safari and iOS manage the extension’s local settings. The developer does not receive those settings.
